## Why kigumi is a hard test

Kigumi is the Japanese tradition of joining wood without nails or glue. The joints have to be cut to tight tolerances
and often fit together in one specific order. If an AI-assisted workflow gets this right, ordinary furniture is easy.

## The gap

Designing is close to solved. Making it physically, on demand, isn't. Big retailers sell the same table to a million
people because that's what their factories and logistics are built for. A local workshop with a CNC can make one table
for one person, but today nobody connects the two.
